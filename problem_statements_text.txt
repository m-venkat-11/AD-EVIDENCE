 Samartha Hackathon — Stage 0
 Research-backed problem statements for Innovation in Education and Brand Marketing
Purpose: This document converts the researched problem spaces into concrete ideas that a student team can
investigate for Stage 0. The emphasis is on real-world pain points, potential AI applications, and prototypes that
can be demonstrated within a 24-hour hackathon.
Research-backed context
 Education
Learning outcomes can vary substantially within the same classroom; teachers have limited capacity for
individualized diagnosis and feedback.
Education + AI
Generative AI can help with tasks, but unguided use can allow students to obtain answers without
necessarily improving learning.
Employability
Employers increasingly emphasize skills, practical evidence and skills assessments, while graduates
can face gaps between academic learning and workplace requirements.
Brand marketing
Marketing data is fragmented across platforms, making measurement and attribution difficult.
Creator marketing
Follower counts alone do not capture creator suitability, audience quality, brand fit or campaign
effectiveness.
AI marketing
AI-generated marketing introduces questions around transparency, claims, brand consistency and
consumer trust.
Consumer discovery
Generative AI is becoming part of shopping and product-research journeys, creating a new surface for
brand visibility.
Theme 1 — Innovation in Education
These problem spaces focus on learning diagnosis, responsible AI-assisted learning, employability, skills evidence
and teacher workload.
E1 — AI Misconception Mapper
 Real-world problem
Most learning systems can identify that an answer is wrong, but they often do not identify the underlying
misconception that caused the mistake. Students in the same class can have very different learning
levels, while teachers have limited time to diagnose each student's reasoning.
Proposed solution
An AI system that analyses answer patterns, code submissions or worked solutions to infer recurring
misconceptions and present a learning map to both student and teacher.
Core workflow
Student answers → mistake-pattern analysis → misconception detection → targeted intervention →
teacher/class heatmap.
24-hour MVP
Start with one subject such as C programming or mathematics. Use a small bank of questions and
common misconception patterns.
E2 — Socratic Learning Agent
 Real-world problem
Students can use generative AI to obtain complete answers to assignments. This can improve task
completion without necessarily improving the student's own understanding.
Proposed solution
A tutoring agent that guides the learner through questions, hints and progressively stronger support
instead of immediately revealing the complete solution.
Core workflow
Student asks → AI estimates understanding → guiding question → student attempt → misconception
detected → hint → learning trail.

24-hour MVP
Build the experience around a small set of mathematics, programming or reasoning problems.
Demonstrate answer generation versus learning guidance.
E3 — AI Curriculum-to-Career Gap Analyzer
 Real-world problem
College curricula and rapidly changing industry skill requirements can diverge. Students and institutions
need a clearer way to understand which skills are covered, missing or becoming important.
Proposed solution
Compare course syllabi and learning outcomes against current job descriptions and industry skill
requirements, then generate a gap map and learning roadmap.
Core workflow
Syllabus + job descriptions → skill extraction → normalization → coverage/gap analysis → student
roadmap + institutional insights.
24-hour MVP
Use a focused domain such as software engineering. Upload a syllabus and job descriptions, extract
skills, and visualize curriculum-versus-market gaps.
E4 — SkillProof: Evidence-Based Skill Verification
 Real-world problem
A certificate or resume claim does not necessarily demonstrate that a student can perform a practical
skill. Employers increasingly use skills assessments and work evidence.
Proposed solution
A system that gives students short practical challenges, observes their work and produces a structured
skill-evidence profile rather than only a certificate.
Core workflow
Skill claim → practical challenge → solution/debugging evidence → competency analysis → verifiable
skill profile.
24-hour MVP
Prototype one skill, such as C programming. Measure correctness, testing, debugging and reasoning,
then generate an evidence report.
E5 — AI Teaching Copilot
 Real-world problem
Teachers face substantial marking and administrative workloads, making individualized feedback difficult
at scale.
Proposed solution
An AI decision-support system that groups students by misconception, summarizes recurring errors and
highlights where teacher intervention can have the greatest effect.
Core workflow
Student submissions → AI analysis → misconception clusters → class heatmap → teacher intervention
suggestions.
24-hour MVP
Use a small classroom dataset. Show how many individual submissions become a simple class-level
intervention dashboard.

Theme 2 — Brand Marketing
These problem spaces focus on measurement, creator intelligence, AI-generated content governance, AI-era
brand visibility, consumer signals and advertising fatigue.
B1 — Cross-Channel Marketing Truth Engine
 Real-world problem
Brands advertise across search, social media, video, influencers, email and websites. Each platform
reports different metrics, making it difficult to connect marketing activity with business outcomes.
Proposed solution
A unified analytics layer that accepts data from multiple channels, normalizes metrics and explains which
activities appear to contribute to conversions or other business outcomes.
Core workflow
CSV/API data → metric normalization → cross-channel analysis → attribution signals → AI explanation.
24-hour MVP
For the hackathon, use CSV uploads or simulated APIs rather than integrating many live platforms. Focus
on the insight layer.
B2 — Creator Intelligence Engine
 Real-world problem
Follower count is an incomplete measure of creator value. Brands also need audience relevance,
engagement quality, brand fit, authenticity, safety and historical campaign evidence.
Proposed solution
Analyze creator profiles and campaign data to produce a structured suitability profile for a particular brand
or campaign.
Core workflow
Creator data → audience/engagement analysis → brand-fit analysis → risk signals → campaign
suitability profile.
24-hour MVP
Create a small synthetic or public-data dataset and demonstrate why creators with different follower
counts can have different campaign suitability.
B3 — Brand Trust Guardian for AI-Generated Marketing
 Real-world problem
Brands can generate large volumes of AI-created copy, images and video, but must still manage
unsupported claims, brand consistency, tone, disclosure and consumer trust.
Proposed solution
A pre-publication AI review layer that checks marketing content against brand guidelines, evidence for
product claims and defined risk rules.
Core workflow
AI-generated content → claim check → brand guideline check → tone/context check → risk report →
human approval.
24-hour MVP
Build a brand rulebook and a small set of marketing assets. Demonstrate how the system catches
deliberately inserted claim or brand-safety problems.
B4 — AI Brand Visibility Monitor
 Real-world problem
Consumers are increasingly using generative AI during product research. Brands therefore have a new
question: how are AI systems representing, comparing and recommending their brand?
Proposed solution
Run standardized consumer-style questions against selected AI systems or stored response datasets
and analyze brand mentions, positioning, attributes and competitor context.
Core workflow
Consumer questions → AI responses → brand/entity extraction → competitor/context analysis → visibility
report.
24-hour MVP
For a 24-hour MVP, use a controlled set of questions and captured responses. Focus on analytics and
insight rather than building a large crawler.

Real-world problem
Consumer interests and complaints can change quickly. Marketing teams need early signals from search
behavior, reviews, social conversations and other feedback channels.
Proposed solution
Aggregate multiple consumer-signal sources and detect emerging themes, changes in sentiment and
potential product/marketing opportunities.
Core workflow
Signals → clustering → trend detection → sentiment/theme analysis → opportunity alert.
24-hour MVP
Start with reviews/comments or a prepared dataset for one category. Demonstrate how many small
signals become one actionable trend.
B6 — Attention-Aware Marketing Engine
 Real-world problem
Ad fatigue and rising acquisition costs create pressure to improve how often and where customers are
exposed to marketing messages.
Proposed solution
Use exposure history, engagement and customer-stage signals to recommend whether to show, delay,
change or stop a marketing message.
Core workflow
Exposure history + engagement + customer stage → fatigue estimation → action recommendation.
24-hour MVP
Use a simulated customer journey and campaign dataset. Demonstrate how the system avoids
repeatedly showing the same message to an overexposed user.
How to select a Stage-0 problem
 Pain
Can we clearly identify who experiences the problem and what it costs them?
Originality
Are we solving a specific gap rather than building a generic chatbot/dashboard?
AI necessity
Does AI perform reasoning, diagnosis, prediction, classification or adaptation that materially helps?
Data
Can we obtain enough real, synthetic or public data to demonstrate the concept?
24-hour feasibility
Can the core workflow be demonstrated convincingly within the hackathon?
Demo clarity
Can a judge understand the value within 30–60 seconds of seeing the prototype?
Scalability
Could the prototype become a real product after the hackathon?
